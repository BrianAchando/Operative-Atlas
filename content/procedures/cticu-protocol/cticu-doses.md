## [cticu-doses-0] Electrolytes: K⁺, Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺, PO₄

Doses as in **Medscape Reference**, the unit standard <span class="tag knh">KNH practice</span>, prepared from KNH stock. Targets are the unit's; limits are the product label's.

#### Potassium: KCl 15% (2 mmol/mL; 10 mL = 20 mmol)

Target after cardiac surgery **4.0–5.0 mmol/L** <span class="tag gl">guideline</span>. Never undiluted, never as a push.

| Serum K⁺ | Maximum rate | Max / 24 h |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.5 or more | 10 mmol/h, at no more than 40 mmol/L | 200 mmol |
| Under 2.0 with ECG changes or paralysis | up to 40 mmol/h, continuous ECG | 400 mmol |

**Peripheral bag**: 10 mL KCl 15% (20 mmol) in 500 mL 0.9% saline = 40 mmol/L; 250 mL/h gives 10 mmol/h. Use saline, not dextrose, when urgent. Hold if urine is under 0.5 mL/kg/h or creatinine is rising. Concentrated central-line syringes: unit to decide <span class="tag prop">proposed</span>

#### Magnesium: MgSO₄

Target after cardiac surgery 1.0 mmol/L or more <span class="tag prop">proposed</span>

| Deficiency | Dose |
| --- | --- |
| Mild | 1 g IM every 6 h for 4 doses |
| Severe | 5 g (about 20 mmol) in 1 L 5% dextrose or 0.9% saline IV over 3 h |

Dilute 50% MgSO₄ to 20% or less for IV use; no faster than 150 mg/min. Severe renal impairment: no more than 20 g in 48 h. Pharmacy to confirm the vial strength stocked.

#### Calcium: calcium gluconate 10% (10 mL = 1 g, about 2.3 mmol Ca)

Target ionized Ca 1.1–1.3 mmol/L.

| Ionized Ca | Dose |
| --- | --- |
| 1.0–1.2 (mild) | 1–2 g IV over 2 h |
| Under 1.0, no tetany | 0.5 mg/kg/h IV, up to 2 mg/kg/h; no more than 3–4 g over 4 h |
| Tetany or seizures | about 3 g (30 mL) IV over 5–10 min, then 0.5 mg/kg/h |

Maximum 50–100 mg/min as a push, 200 mg/min as an infusion. Not in the same line as bicarbonate or phosphate. Pharmacy to confirm whether the mg/kg/h rate is elemental calcium.

#### Phosphate

| Serum PO₄ | Dose, IV over 4–6 h |
| --- | --- |
| Under 0.16 mmol/L (0.5 mg/dL) | 0.5 mmol/kg |
| 0.16–0.32 mmol/L (0.5–1 mg/dL) | 0.25 mmol/kg |

Potassium phosphate if K⁺ is low, sodium phosphate otherwise; caution in renal impairment. Milder deficits: unit to set <span class="tag prop">proposed</span>

> **Evidence:** Medscape Reference (calcium gluconate, sodium phosphates IV); potassium chloride and magnesium sulfate prescribing information (product labels, as followed by Medscape).

## [cticu-doses-1] Noradrenaline and adrenaline infusions

**One standard syringe for both**: 4 mL of the 1 mg/mL vial (4 mg) + 46 mL 5% dextrose = 50 mL at **80 µg/mL**, central line only. Label clearly: the two syringes look the same. The choice of drug and dose is the anesthetist's <span class="tag knh">KNH practice</span>

#### Noradrenaline

Start 8–12 µg/min and titrate to MAP; maintenance usually 2–4 µg/min. **mL/h = µg/min × 0.75**

#### Adrenaline

0.05–2 µg/kg/min, titrated to MAP by 0.05–0.2 µg/kg/min every 10–15 min; wean every 30 min over 12–24 h once stable. **mL/h = µg/kg/min × kg × 0.75**

| kg | 0.02 | 0.05 | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 50 | 0.8 | 1.9 | 3.8 | 7.5 | 18.8 |
| 60 | 0.9 | 2.3 | 4.5 | 9.0 | 22.5 |
| 70 | 1.1 | 2.6 | 5.3 | 10.5 | 26.3 |
| 80 | 1.2 | 3.0 | 6.0 | 12.0 | 30.0 |
| 90 | 1.4 | 3.4 | 6.8 | 13.5 | 33.8 |

<div class="box red"><h4>Call the consultant</h4><ul><li>Rising noradrenaline need: look for bleeding, tamponade, sepsis</li><li>Rising adrenaline need: echo for tamponade, RV or graft failure; consider an IABP</li></ul></div>

> **Evidence:** Medscape Reference: norepinephrine (initial 8–12 µg/min, maintenance 2–4 µg/min) and epinephrine (0.05–2 µg/kg/min in shock).
