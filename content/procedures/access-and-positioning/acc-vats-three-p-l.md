## [vmthree-pl-map] Three-port VATS, posterior (fissure) approach

**Utility incision** in the **5th space** in line with the fissure; **camera** low in the posterior axillary line; **working port** posteriorly, below the scapular tip. Fissure first, artery first.

The posterior ports go through latissimus dorsi as well as serratus anterior.

## [vmthree-pl-layers] What the port goes through

Skin and subcutaneous fat → Latissimus dorsi: passed through → Serratus anterior: passed through → Intercostal muscles, on the upper border of the rib below.

Enter on the **upper border of the lower rib**; open the pleura with a finger and sweep for adhesions before the first instrument goes in.

## [vmthree-pl-inside] The view from inside

From the camera the hilum sits in the middle of the screen, the instruments entering from separate ports at an angle to the camera (triangulation).
